Part 4: Practical Modeling: Moore’s Law Calculator 25 Pts

In Lecture 1, you explored Moore’s Law, which models the historical observation that the number of transistors on microchips roughly doubles every 2 years:

N(t) = N0 × 2t - t02

Where:

  • t0 = 1971 (Release year of the Intel 4004 microprocessor)
  • N0 = 2,300 (Transistor count of the Intel 4004)
  • t = Target analysis year entered by the user (t ≥ 1971)

Implementation Requirements:

  1. Prompt the user to enter a target year t as an integer.
  2. Calculate the estimated number of transistors N(t) using the formula.
  3. Calculate the Growth Factor relative to 1971:
Growth Factor = N(t)N0
  1. Display the target year, predicted transistor count, and growth factor (formatted with 2 decimal places).
